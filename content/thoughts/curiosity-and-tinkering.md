## A small philosophy

A lot of the work I care about starts with a tiny question: how could this be better, simpler, or more comfortable? Not every idea becomes a finished project, but the act of experimenting is often where the real insight appears.

I like tools that make it easy to try things quickly, observe what breaks, and adjust from there. That mindset applies just as much to software and hardware as it does to the way I think about learning and building a life around creative work.

## Why it matters

Curiosity is rarely a dramatic leap. It is usually a series of small observations, repeated experiments, and the willingness to keep going after the first version is only partially useful. The goal is not to prove a grand theory immediately, but to learn what is actually worth pursuing.

That is the part I enjoy most: following a thread far enough to understand it, then deciding whether it deserves another round of refinement.
